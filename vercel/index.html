About

This is a simple web application that allows you to draw on a canvas and recognizes your handwriting. It uses a machine learning model to interpret the strokes and provide feedback on what the model thinks you wrote.

The model was trained on the MNIST dataset and has an accuracy of +98% on the test set. Despite this, the model may not always recognize your handwriting correctly, especially if it is messy or unconventional.

More information about the model at here.

How to use ?

  • Draw a digit (0-9) on the canvas.
  • Left click to draw on the canvas.
  • Right click to erase from the canvas.
  • Press "c" to clear the canvas.
